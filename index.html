Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all tours completely private? +
Yes. Every experience is organized exclusively for you and your travel companions—whether you are traveling solo, as a couple, with family, or in a small private group.
Which languages do you guide in? +
I offer private guiding in English, Slovenian, Croatian, German, and Spanish. Simply let me know your preferred language when reaching out.
Can we customize the itinerary and duration? +
Absolutely. We can adjust the starting time, walking pace, stops, and focus areas (history, nature, photography, or local food) to fit your schedule.
How do I check availability or book a date? +
Just send me a message via email or WhatsApp with your preferred dates, number of people, and general interests. I will get back to you personally with ideas and availability.
